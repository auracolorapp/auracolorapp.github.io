Welcome to AURA ("Application" or "AURA"). This Privacy Policy describes how the AURA team ("Developer", "we", "us", or "our") collects, processes, protects, and handles your personal information when you use our mobile application and related services. This document aims to establish clear, legally binding terms that protect both the Developer and our users.

✦ Image Processing, Photos, and Camera Policy: AURA utilizes your device camera and photo library access solely to perform seasonal color analysis. All processing of uploaded photos is performed locally on-device. Photos are immediately deleted from device memory once the analysis is completed. We do not transmit, save, store, or share your photographs to any server or third-party entity. Furthermore, our image analysis does not collect biometric data, facial signatures, or face geometry for personal identification purposes.

1. Types of Data We Collect and Why

To provide and maintain our services, we collect the following minimal categories of data:

  • User Account Information: If you sign up using Google Sign-In or Apple Sign-In, we collect the unique user ID (UID), full name, and email address shared by these providers. This data is stored in our database in an encrypted format to create, authenticate, and manage your AURA account.
  • Color Analysis History: To ensure the persistence of your experience, your saved seasonal color palettes, custom color swatches, and wardrobe counts are stored in our secure database linked to your account.
  • Device and Usage Information: Technical data such as your device model, operating system version, device language, push notification tokens, and crash logs are collected anonymously to analyze application performance, fix bugs, and optimize stability.

AURA integrates trusted third-party service providers to power its database and payment operations. These providers govern your data under their respective privacy policies, and the Developer is not responsible for their independent data processing practices:
