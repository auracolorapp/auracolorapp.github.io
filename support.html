Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

✦ How does AURA's color analysis work?

AURA uses advanced image processing algorithms based on the classic 12-season Munsell color system. By analyzing the contrast and undertones between your skin, eyes, and hair in a selfie taken in natural light, it matches you to one of the 12 seasonal palettes.

✦ Is my photo data uploaded to any servers?

No. To ensure absolute privacy, all photo analysis is processed locally on your device. Your photo is immediately purged from memory once the color season is detected. We never store or transmit your photos.

✦ How do I restore my premium purchases?

If you switch devices or need to restore your subscription status, open the AURA application, navigate to **Profile > Settings > Subscription Ledger**, and tap **Restore Purchases**. Your status will be instantly verified via the App Store.

✦ How can I delete my account and data?

You can delete your account and all associated profile history instantly by opening AURA, navigating to **Settings**, and selecting **Delete Account**. Alternatively, you can request manual data deletion by emailing auracolorapp@gmail.com.
